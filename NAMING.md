# Naming and publication research — preliminary

**Checked October 23, 2026. Working names: .GPT, GPTFS, GPT File System.**
This is a research checkpoint, not clearance or a conclusion about registration rights.

- [OpenAI's brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) identify GPT among its
  brands. Treat that as a concrete reason to review the proposed public branding;
  do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
- [USPTO clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)
  describes a broader search than an exact-name database lookup, including similar
  marks and other uses. No comprehensive registry/class/jurisdiction search has been
  completed in this project.
- GitHub's live name search returned [devoc09/gptfs](https://github.com/devoc09/gptfs),
  [pstringe/GPTfs-gui](https://github.com/pstringe/GPTfs-gui), and
  [MorenoLand/Moreno.GPTFS](https://github.com/MorenoLand/Moreno.GPTFS).
  These are third-party naming leads, not Drew's repository. Their existence does
  not establish infringement or semantic equivalence.

Before public naming: inspect competing uses, search relevant registries and software
package indexes, review file-extension collisions, consider distinctive alternatives,
record intended jurisdictions/goods/services, and obtain Drew's recorded naming
decision. Keep internal protocol identifiers versioned so a public rename need not
break stored identities. Do not publish a standard or branded release on the basis
of this preliminary search alone.
